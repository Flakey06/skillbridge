How evidence weighting works

Evidence contributes up to 30 points of the candidate’s 100-point match score. Skills contribute the other 70 points.

  1. 1st: 50% of the evidence score — up to 15 points.
  2. 2nd: 33.3% — up to 10 points.
  3. 3rd: 16.7% — up to 5 points.

A candidate earns each portion when that evidence is found in their profile or résumé. Ranking changes importance; it does not guarantee that points are awarded.
