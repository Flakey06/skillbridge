How the fixed ATS score works

The ATS score is graded manually by a recruiter—not generated by AI. Open a candidate and complete the Structured ATS evaluation survey.

  1. Role-specific skills: evidence that the candidate has the skills required by this listing.
  2. Relevant experience: how closely their work, projects, or education relate to the role.
  3. Supporting evidence: the quality and clarity of examples in their application and resume.
  4. Role readiness: how prepared they appear to perform the role at its stated level.

Each answer is graded from 1 to 5. The four answers total a maximum of 20 points and are converted using points ÷ 20 × 100.

Example: 16 points becomes 80/100. A minimum filter of 80+ shows evaluated candidates scoring 80 or higher. Candidates marked “Not scored” are excluded.
