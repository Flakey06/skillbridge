How the ATS score works

Close

The ATS score is calculated automatically when the candidate applies. It compares evidence saved in the candidate profile and résumé with the requirements of that specific job.

  1. Required skills: skills found in the candidate profile or résumé.
  2. Preferred skills: additional matching skills requested by the recruiter.
  3. Experience and evidence: relevant work, education, certifications, projects, and résumé content.
  4. Career alignment: how closely the candidate’s direction matches the role.

The result is saved with the application on a scale of 0–100, so recruiters see the same submission-time assessment even if the candidate later edits their profile.

The minimum ATS filter now uses this automatic score. Recruiters can still complete a separate structured evaluation from the candidate detail page.
